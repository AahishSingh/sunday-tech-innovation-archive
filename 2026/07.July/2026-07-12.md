# Weekly Tech Innovation Update — 2026-07-12

## Major Headlines

* **OpenAI publicly launches the GPT-5.6 family** (Sol, Terra, and Luna), introduces **ChatGPT Work**, and unifies ChatGPT and Codex into a single desktop application.
* **Apple sues OpenAI** over alleged theft of confidential hardware trade secrets by former Apple employees now working at OpenAI.
* **Meta rapidly expands its AI ecosystem**, releasing Muse Image, Muse Video previews, Muse Spark 1.1, and a new Meta Model API while facing immediate privacy backlash and EU scrutiny.
* **SK Hynix completes the largest U.S. IPO ever by a foreign company**, raising **$26.5B**, reinforcing AI memory's dominance in global markets.
* AI infrastructure spending continues accelerating as **Amazon, Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Apple** commit tens of billions toward chips, data centers, and AI compute.
* Governments worldwide intensify AI oversight through export controls, AI safety legislation, and investigations into frontier AI models.
