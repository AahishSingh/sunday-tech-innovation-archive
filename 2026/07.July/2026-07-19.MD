## Artificial Intelligence

### OpenAI

- Apple issues legal preservation notices to approximately 40 former Apple employees now working at OpenAI as the hardware trade-secret lawsuit expands.
- Continues defending itself against Apple's allegations while pursuing its AI hardware roadmap.
- Faces increasing competition from Anthropic, Meta, Google, and rapidly advancing Chinese AI labs.

### Anthropic

- Reportedly negotiating a **~$10B, two-year compute leasing agreement** with Meta.
- Claude Fable 5 continues driving strong enterprise demand, increasing competitive pricing pressure across frontier AI providers.
- Remains one of the largest consumers of AI compute infrastructure globally.

### Meta

- In talks to lease AI computing capacity from its hyperscale data centers to Anthropic in what could become one of the largest AI infrastructure agreements ever.
- Recruits former AWS executive **Dave Brown** to lead and accelerate Meta's AI data center expansion.
- Continues evolving from an AI model developer into a major AI cloud infrastructure provider.

### Google

- Faces renewed regulatory scrutiny in Europe.
- Continues expanding AI products while addressing labor concerns and regulatory challenges.

### Microsoft

- Preparing a new enterprise AI cybersecurity platform powered by Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic models.

### Apple

- Expands legal action against OpenAI through formal document preservation notices.
- Begins early discussions with the U.S. Department of Justice regarding a possible settlement of its 2024 antitrust lawsuit.
- Raises Apple Music subscription prices.
