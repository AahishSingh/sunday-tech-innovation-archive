# Weekly Tech Innovation Update — 2026-06-28

## Major Headlines

* OpenAI unveils the **GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, and Luna)**, though its release is limited by U.S. government security reviews.
* The U.S. partially lifts restrictions on **Anthropic's Mythos 5**, allowing deployment to more than 100 U.S. institutions while Fable 5 remains under review.
* OpenAI and Broadcom introduce **Jalapeño**, a custom AI inference chip designed and taped out in just nine months.
