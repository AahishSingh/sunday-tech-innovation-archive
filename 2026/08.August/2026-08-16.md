## Artificial Intelligence

- Anthropic announces that Auto mode will become the default in Claude Code for Pro, Max, and Team plans starting August 14.
- Anthropic says its internal study with 1,053 paid testers found that humans detected dangerous commands only 13.6% of the time initially, falling to around 5% after repeated exposure, while Auto mode blocked the same dangerous commands approximately 89% of the time.
- Anthropic's Claude Code team says it has been using Auto mode internally for months and believes it is more effective than repeatedly asking users to approve individual commands.
- Critics argue that an 89% blocking rate still leaves an unacceptable failure rate and that AI coding environments should rely on stronger sandboxing and system-level boundaries rather than human approval prompts alone.
- Z.ai releases GLM-5.3, using the same base model as GLM-5.2 with scaled post-training aimed at improving coding and reasoning performance.
- Z.ai says GLM-5.3 scores 84.5% on CyberGym, compared with 83.8% for Mythos 5, while restricting its most sensitive cybersecurity capabilities to verified users.
- GLM-5.3 reportedly identifies a potentially serious vulnerability in Cursor during a reverse-engineering task, which Z.ai says was privately disclosed to Cursor.
- Alibaba releases the weights of Qwen3.8 models under the Apache 2.0 license, including Qwen3.8-27B.
- Qwen3.8-27B reportedly delivers significant improvements over the previous generation, including large gains on terminal coding, SWE-bench Pro, DeepSWE, and software-engineering benchmarks.
- Developers report that Qwen3.8-27B can be run locally on relatively affordable consumer hardware, further narrowing the gap between frontier proprietary models and open-weight systems.
- Google adds an option in Gemini and Flow to remove visible watermarks from AI-generated images, video, and music, while retaining SynthID watermarks and C2PA metadata.
- OpenAI's leadership reportedly becomes more involved in preparations for a potential IPO, with Greg Brockman taking a more active role across the organization.
- Former and current OpenAI employees reportedly say pressure to rapidly release AI models and products has reduced the amount of time available for safety work.
- Nvidia reportedly restructures financing for an OpenAI Ohio data-center project, initially guaranteeing only half of a planned $250B backstop.
- DeepSeek releases DeepSeek Harness, an MIT-licensed developer-preview agent framework built around an "everything is a plugin" architecture.
- OpenAI's enterprise business reportedly surpasses its consumer business in revenue, with enterprise customers increasing 32% in July.
- Anthropic reportedly tells investors it generated more than $11.5B in Q2 revenue, compared with $4.73B in Q1 and $787M in Q2 2025.
- Anthropic raises its estimated misalignment risk from "very low" to "low" and says it does not plan to release a stronger internal model known as "Model 2."
- Apple reportedly worked with Alibaba to train a China-specific LLM, which has been registered with China's Cyberspace Administration.
- Alibaba's involvement could allow Apple to provide a localized proprietary AI experience in China despite the country's regulatory requirements.
- Google appears increasingly focused on AI diffusion and infrastructure opportunities alongside frontier-model development.
- AI adoption continues spreading into enterprise workflows, cybersecurity, coding, manufacturing, payments, and consumer products.
